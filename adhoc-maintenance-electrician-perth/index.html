Ad-Hoc Maintenance Services

Every Electrical Fault, Diagnosed and Fixed

From nuisance trips to major power failures. Responsive service, complete certainty on every job, lasting repairs.

Nuisance Circuit Breaker Trips

Circuit breaker keeps tripping? We find what's causing it - overloaded circuit, leaking appliance, or fault - and fix the root cause, not just reset the breaker.

Power Outages

Section of the building without power? We systematically test circuits, isolators, and cable to locate the fault - open circuit, short, or protection device failure.

Lighting Failures

Light circuit out? We check the switch, wiring, fuse, and fitting. Quick diagnosis, quick fix - usually same-day resolution.

Appliance Faults

Appliance not working? We test it, check the circuit, and either repair or replace as needed. If it's the building, we fix the wiring. If it's the appliance, we advise on replacement.

Safety Switch Tripping

Safety switch (RCD) keeps tripping? Earth leakage somewhere. We find it - usually a failing appliance or moisture in cables - and fix it properly.

Emergency Response

After-hours fault? We have 24/7 response for genuine emergencies. Call us and we'll get an electrician to you as fast as possible.

Electrical faults cost money in lost productivity. Every hour your business is without power or light is revenue lost. Fast diagnosis and repair matter.
