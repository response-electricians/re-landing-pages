Complete Solutions

Exhaust Fan Services

Six types of exhaust ventilation installations, all meeting Australian building codes.

Bathroom Exhaust Fans

NCC 25 L/s minimum extraction. We mount IP-rated fans, run ducting to the roof or external wall, and prevent moisture buildup that causes mould.

Kitchen Rangehood Ducting

NCC 50 L/s extraction. We install insulated rigid ducts that eliminate cooking odours and steam properly. No recirculating fans where ducted is needed.

Laundry Exhaust Fans

NCC 20 L/s minimum. Extract moisture from clothes dryers and laundries so damp doesn't travel into living areas or cause structural damage.

Roof-Mounted Exhaust Systems

High-extraction fans mounted at roof level with dampers and insulated ducting. Ideal for large spaces, commercial kitchens, and whole-building ventilation.

Inline Exhaust Fans

High-performance fans that mount remotely in roof spaces or walls. Quieter than ceiling fans and suitable for distribution to multiple outlets.

Exhaust Fan Replacement & Upgrade

Old fans not extracting properly? We upgrade to modern, quieter, more efficient models with greater extraction rates and smart controls.

Exhaust Fan Installation FAQ

Australian building code (NCC Part 10.6) requires a minimum of 25 litres per second extraction for bathrooms. We size fans based on room volume and moisture load to ensure effective moisture removal.
Kitchen rangehoods require a minimum of 50 litres per second extraction. We assess your kitchen layout, cooking intensity, and ducting requirements to recommend the right system.

Ceiling-mounted fans are standard for most spaces. Inline fans mount remotely (in roof or wall) for higher extraction rates and quieter operation, ideal for larger areas or commercial applications.
Exhaust must exit to the outside. Ducting into roof cavities causes moisture buildup and roof damage. We run ducts to roof penetrations or external wall vents with proper sealing and insulation.
