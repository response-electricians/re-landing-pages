How It All Started

One Van. One Mission.
Make Perth Safer.

In 2009, Greg Allan loaded up a van and started Response Electricians with one straightforward goal: to make every home in Western Australia safer. What began as one man on the road has grown into Perth's most trusted electrical business, but the mission hasn't changed.

Greg developed what became Australia's most comprehensive residential Electrical Health Check, recognised nationally and still the gold standard today. As the business grew, so did Greg's commitment to lifting the entire trade. He was elected as a National Director and Board Member of Master Electricians Australia, and sits on the National Electrical Technical Committee, giving back to the industry that built him.

Today, Response Electricians has served over 80,000 Perth homes and businesses. We've earned more five-star reviews than any electrical business in Australia, and we've won the national Electrician of the Year award twice. But the thing we're most proud of? Our customers keep coming back, and they keep sending their friends.

Greg Allan · Founder
