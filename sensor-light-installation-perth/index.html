What We Install

Sensor Light Services

From a single driveway sensor to a full property security system. We position lights for complete coverage with no dark spots.

Motion Sensor Flood Lights

Bright LED flood lights that turn on when movement is detected. Ideal for driveways, carports, and security.

Dusk-to-Dawn Lights

Automatic lights that turn on at sunset and off at sunrise. Perfect for continuous security without manual control.

Security Entry Sensors

Motion-activated lights for front doors, side gates, and entry points. Deter unwanted visitors and light your way home.

Driveway & Pathway Lights

Motion or dusk-to-dawn sensors for driveways, pathways, and gardens. Safe, attractive lighting that improves home security.

Commercial Car Park Sensors

High-powered motion sensors for car parks, warehouses, and commercial properties. Efficient lighting that saves on energy costs.

Sensor Control Upgrades

Replace old lights with smart sensors. Adjust timing, sensitivity, and brightness remotely for maximum control.
