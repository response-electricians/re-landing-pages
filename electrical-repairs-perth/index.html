Common Electrical Repairs

What We Fix

The most common electrical faults in Perth homes, and how we solve them.

Flickering Lights

Loose connections, faulty dimmers, or failing bulbs. We find the cause and fix it permanently so your lights stay steady.

Learn More →

Tripping Circuit Breakers

Overloaded circuits or short circuits cause breakers to trip. We identify what's overloading the circuit and fix it so it stops happening.

Learn More →

Faulty Power Points

Loose plugs, burn marks, or outlets that won't hold plugs. We replace them safely and make sure they're properly earthed.

Learn More →

Damaged Wiring

Chewed cables, burnt insulation, or water-damaged wiring. We isolate the fault and run new cable to code. Permanent fix, stays safe.

Learn More →

Burnt or Blackened Outlets

Scorched power points are a fire hazard. We replace them and check what caused the burn so it doesn't happen again.

Learn More →

Appliance Circuit Faults

Your kettle, oven, or heater trips the power? We test the appliance, check the circuit, and either fix the wiring or recommend replacement.

Questions Answered

Electrical Repairs FAQ

Flickering can be caused by loose connections, faulty dimmers, failing bulbs, or issues in the switchboard. We test the circuit and find the exact cause.
Breakers trip as a safety feature when circuits are overloaded or when a short circuit occurs. We identify what's causing the overload and fix it so it doesn't keep happening.
Warning signs include burn marks around the outlet, loose plugs that fall out easily, discolouration, or plugs that won't stay in. Stop using them and call us for replacement.
Damaged wiring must be replaced. Patching is not code-compliant and is dangerous. We isolate the damaged section and run new cable to electrical standards.
We talk you through the job and give you a clear plan before we attend. Most repairs range from $250-$800 depending on complexity. Power point replacements start from $150 each.
