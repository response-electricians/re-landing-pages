Complete Solutions

Bathroom Electrical Services

Six core services that make bathrooms safe, comfortable, and compliant to code.

Exhaust Fans (25 L/s Compliant)

Moisture extraction to Australian building code. We specify the right fan for your bathroom size and ensure proper ducting to prevent condensation and mould.

Heated Towel Rails

Dedicated circuits with proper earthing and protection. We install 1000W to 2000W towel rails safely and connect them to thermostat controls if needed.

LED Downlights & Mirror Lights

IP-rated downlights (IP44 minimum for wet areas, IP65 for showers). LED options with dimmers, colour temperature selection, and integrated mirror lighting.

Safe Zone Power Points

AS/NZS 3000 compliant outlets in zones 1 and 2. We specify shaver sockets, weatherproof covers for wet areas, and proper circuit protection.

Heat Lamps & Infrared Heaters

Instant bathroom warmth. We install infrared heat lamps and electric heaters on dedicated circuits, ensuring safe installation in moisture-rich environments.

Bathroom Renovation Rewire

Complete rewiring for renovations. New circuits, updated positions, code-compliant safety zones, and modern fixtures. We plan the layout and deliver fixed pricing.

Your bathroom deserves proper electrical design. Code-compliant, future-proof, and built by licensed electricians.
