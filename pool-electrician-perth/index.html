Why Choose Response

Licensed Electricians, Safety First

Proper Isolation & GFCI Protection

Pool lighting requires isolation transformers and ground fault circuit interrupters (GFCI). We install them correctly so you and your family are completely safe from electrical hazards.

Compliant With Australian Standards

Every pool lighting installation meets AS3000, the Australian standard for electrical installations. Your installation is certified, safe, and covered by warranty.

Energy-Efficient LED Systems

12-volt LED lights use a fraction of the power of older halogen systems. Lower energy bills, less heat, better light. Eco-friendly and beautiful.

Design, Supply & Install

We design your custom lighting layout, source quality components, and install everything professionally. One point of contact, one warranty, complete peace of mind.
