Garden Lighting FAQ

Pathway lighting: $200-$500. Deck or patio area: $500-$1,200. Pool area with multiple spotlights: $800-$2,000. LED upgrades to existing systems: $300-$800. Depends on length, number of fixtures, and whether we are upgrading or starting fresh.
12V systems use a transformer to convert household 240V power to safe low-voltage 12V. This means thinner cables, easier installation, no risk of electric shock, and lower running costs. Perfect for gardens, decks, and outdoor spaces.
Havit is the standard for Australian residential garden lighting. Superior colour rendering (warm 3000K for ambience), durable stainless steel construction, IP66 waterproof rating, and 15-20 year lifespan. No glare, low maintenance, and designed for Perth climate.
Yes. Every garden lighting installation includes timer setup. Lights turn on automatically at dusk (sunset time varies by season) and off at midnight. No manual switches needed. Smart control via your phone is also available for an additional cost.
Quality LED fittings like Havit last 15-20 years or more. The transformer (power supply) typically lasts 10-15 years. Cables buried in garden soil last for decades. With proper installation and maintenance, your system will serve your garden for well over a decade.
Yes. If your existing garden lighting still has a functioning transformer and cabling, we can replace the old halogen or incandescent fixtures with modern Havit LED heads. It is cost-effective, uses far less power, and transforms the quality of your garden lighting.
