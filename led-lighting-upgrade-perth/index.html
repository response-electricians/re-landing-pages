LED Upgrade Services

Every LED Upgrade, Done Properly

From a handful of halogen replacements to a full commercial refit, we handle the upgrade so you start saving immediately.

Halogen to LED Conversion

Replace old halogen downlights with modern LED units. Lower power consumption, less heat, and better light quality. Most homes done in half a day.

LED Downlight Installation

New LED downlights in any room. We handle the wiring, transformer removal, and proper fitting to Australian standards.

LED Panel Lights

Flat panel LED lights for offices, kitchens, and commercial spaces. Even light distribution, low profile, and energy efficient.

Dimmer-Compatible LEDs

Not all LEDs work with existing dimmers. We match the right LED to your dimmer, or upgrade the dimmer to a trailing-edge model for smooth control.

Colour Temperature Selection

Warm white, cool white, or daylight? We help you choose the right colour temperature for each room and install consistently throughout.

Commercial LED Upgrades

Fluorescent tube replacement, high-bay LED conversion, and office lighting upgrades. Reduce energy costs and improve working conditions.

Old halogens waste 90% of their energy as heat. An LED upgrade pays for itself in lower power bills within months.
