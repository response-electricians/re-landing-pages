What Can We Help Your Business With?

Commercial Electrical Services That Keep You Running

One call gets you access to every commercial electrical service your Noranda business needs. Here's what we do most.

Commercial Fit-Outs & Renovations

Whether you're fitting out a new office on Old Perth Road or renovating a Noranda warehouse, we handle the full electrical scope. Power distribution, lighting layout, data points, and compliance certification. We work around your tenants and your timeline to keep disruption to an absolute minimum.

Learn more →

Lighting Design & LED Upgrades

Poor lighting costs you in energy bills, staff productivity, and customer impressions. We design and install LED systems that transform your workspace — warehouses, retail floors, office fitouts, car parks. You'll notice the difference on your next power bill and your team will notice it straight away.

Learn more →

Switchboard Upgrades & 3-Phase Power

If your switchboard can't keep up with your equipment, you're losing money every time it trips. We upgrade commercial switchboards to handle your actual load, install 3-phase power for heavy machinery, and make sure your RCDs and safety switches are compliant. No shortcuts, no guesswork.

Learn more →

Emergency & Exit Lighting Compliance

Australian standards require regular testing and maintenance of emergency and exit lighting. We install, test, and certify your systems to AS 2293 so you stay compliant and your people stay safe. We'll set up a maintenance schedule that means you never have to think about it again.

Learn more →

Data & Communications Cabling

Structured data cabling, server room fit-outs, network points, and cable management. We install Cat6 and fibre to Australian standards so your network runs clean and your IT team doesn't have to troubleshoot bad runs. Neat, labelled, and documented.

Learn more →

Electrical Maintenance & Testing

Test and tag, RCD testing, thermal imaging, and scheduled maintenance programs that keep your business compliant and your equipment running. We'll build a maintenance plan around your operation so nothing catches you off guard. Prevention is always cheaper than repair.
