Our Dimmer Services

Complete Dimmer Solutions

From simple single-switch replacements to multi-room smart installations, we handle every type of dimmer. All work comes with our lifetime warranty.

LED-Compatible Dimmers

Modern trailing-edge dimmers that work perfectly with LED bulbs. No flickering, no buzzing, smooth dimming from 10% to 100%.

Multi-Gang Dimmers

Control multiple lights or zones from a single dimmer switch. Perfect for open-plan kitchens, living areas, and bedrooms.

Smart Dimmers

Wi-Fi and Zigbee-enabled dimmers for total control from your phone. Create scenes, set schedules, voice control with Alexa or Google.

Fan Speed Controllers

Dedicated switches for ceiling fans that let you adjust both fan speed and light brightness independently.

Dimmer Replacement and Upgrade

Old dimmer flickering or not working with LEDs? We replace it with a modern LED-compatible dimmer. Same hole in the wall, same cable.
