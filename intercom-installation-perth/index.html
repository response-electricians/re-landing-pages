Video Intercom Services

Every Door Entry System, Installed Right

From simple audio intercoms to full IP video systems with smartphone access and gate release control.

Audio Intercoms

Classic intercom system - speak to visitors through a speaker without opening the door. Wired throughout the house or office. Affordable and reliable.

Video Door Intercoms

Aiphone and DoorBird video intercoms let you see the visitor on a screen or your phone before buzzing them in. Crystal-clear night vision. IP67 weatherproof.

Multi-Unit Systems

Apartment blocks and townhouses need access control - individual apartment cameras, lobby monitors, and gate release buttons. Comelit and DoorBird handle complex setups.

Gate Access Control

Electric gate release with video intercom - you see the visitor on your phone, press to unlock the gate remotely. Perfect for driveways and secure compounds.

Smartphone Integration

All modern intercoms now push video and audio to your phone. Answer from work, from holiday, from bed - see who's at the door anywhere in Australia.

Wiring & Power Supply

We run intercom cable through walls, integrate with switchboards, and provide dedicated power supplies. Professional installation means the system lasts 10+ years.

How We Work

Our Process

1

You Call, We Quote

Tell us about your property - gate, multiple doors, apartments, what you want to control. We'll recommend the right system and talk you through the job and give you a clear plan.

2

On-Site Assessment

Our technician checks entry points, measures cable runs, tests internet if you want smartphone access, and confirms gate release location. The price is locked in.

3

Installation Day

Door stations mounted, intercom cable run through walls, gate release wired and tested, and smartphone access configured. Usually done in 4–6 hours.

4

Tested & Ready

We test video quality, gate release operation, smartphone app access, and night vision. Provide login credentials and train you on the system. Lifetime warranty issued.
