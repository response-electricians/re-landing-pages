DC Fast Charging Services

Rapid Charging Infrastructure Built Right

From single 50kW units to multi-unit 350kW fast-charging networks.

50kW–150kW Chargers

Medium-duty rapid chargers for fleet vehicles and commercial operations. Full charge in 30–45 minutes. Suitable for most commercial vehicle types.

200kW–350kW Ultrafast

Premium rapid charging for modern EV fleets. 80% charge in 15–20 minutes. Latest CHAdeMO and CCS Combo 2 connectors supported.

Multi-Unit Networks

Install two or more DC chargers at high-traffic locations. Intelligent power sharing ensures consistent performance during simultaneous charging.

Grid Coordination

Cooling & Infrastructure

High-power DC chargers generate heat. We design proper cooling systems and sturdy installations that withstand 24/7 operation and Perth's climate.

Remote Monitoring

All chargers connected to a central monitoring platform. Track usage, revenue, fault alerts, and maintenance schedules in real time from your office.

How We Work

Our Process

1

Site Assessment & Design

We survey your location, assess grid capacity, and design the electrical infrastructure needed. Detailed proposal delivered within a week.
