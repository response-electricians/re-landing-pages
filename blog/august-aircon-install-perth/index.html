The Perth aircon demand curve

The pattern is the same every year: across Perth, aircon install demand more than doubles between August and November as the weather warms up.

The mechanic is simple. Perth homeowners think about air conditioning when they feel hot. The first proper 32-degree day is when the phones ring. By then, the install teams — ours and every other Perth electrical contractor’s — are running two-week booking windows minimum. By late October, four-week windows are common. By early December, "can you install before Christmas?" is a request we frequently can’t say yes to.

The consequence: customers who book in August choose their install date. Customers who call in December wait.

Choosing the right split system size for a Perth room

Sizing matters more than brand. An undersized unit runs constantly and never quite cools the space; an oversized unit cycles on and off too quickly to dehumidify properly and costs more upfront and to run.

Rough Perth guide (single-storey, insulated brick veneer, reasonable ceiling height):

Room type & size Recommended capacity
Bedroom (10–15m²)2.5kW
Small living / kids room (15–25m²)3.5kW
Medium living (25–40m²)5.0kW
Large living or open-plan (40–60m²)7.0–8.0kW
Extra-large open-plan (60m²+)Ducted or multi-split conversation

Adjustments that shift the number: high ceilings (add 15-20%), poor insulation (add 10-15%), west-facing glass without shading (add 15-25%), open-plan kitchens with cooking heat (add 10%). We factor all of this in when we size the unit over the phone.

What "electrician-led install" actually means

Aircon installation sits on the boundary between two trades: refrigeration mechanic (handles the gas, the refrigerant lines, the compressor) and licensed electrician (handles the electrical side). The best installs have both properly qualified — not a mechanic operating on a limited electrical licence, and not an electrician improvising the refrigerant work.

Our installs include the full electrical scope, done properly:

  • Dedicated circuit back to the switchboard. Aircon load doesn’t share with power points or lights.
  • Clipsal double-pole isolator mounted beside the outdoor unit. Required by AS/NZS 3000, sometimes skipped by cheap installs.
  • RCD protection on the circuit where required.
  • Correct cable size for the unit’s current draw — usually 2.5mm² or 4mm² depending on the model.
  • Full electrical certification — Notice of Completion + Electrical Safety Certificate issued directly by us, on the day.

The refrigeration side (mounting the indoor head, running the refrigerant lines with black foam insulation, evacuating and charging the system, commissioning) is done by our own qualified refrigeration mechanic in the same visit. One company, one job, one warranty.

TCL, Mitsubishi Electric & Daikin: our good, better, best line-up

We install three brands as standard — TCL, Mitsubishi Electric and Daikin. Same electrical scope, same fixed-price process, same lifetime workmanship guarantee. The choice comes down to budget, features, and where the unit is going.

TCL — Good (value tier)

  • Best value on the install — the lowest entry point for a properly installed inverter split.
  • Solid modern inverter technology — efficient running costs, quiet enough for most rooms.
  • Great for rentals, secondary bedrooms, and budget-conscious upgrades where the unit needs to work without breaking the bank.
  • Full AU warranty and increasingly good parts availability locally.

Mitsubishi Electric — Better (workhorse mid-tier)

  • Reliability sweet spot — the MSZ range is the classic Perth workhorse.
  • Excellent parts availability in WA if a repair is ever needed years later.
  • Simpler controls — matters if the aircon is going into a rental or grandparents’ place.
  • Mid-range asking price with premium-adjacent build quality.

Daikin — Best (premium tier)

  • Quietest at the top end — Cora and US7 series in particular. Noticeable in bedrooms.
  • Better dehumidification — a small edge for Perth’s humid summer weeks.
  • Streamer discharge technology on premium models — some genuine anti-bacterial benefit.
  • Highest price point at each capacity tier — buying the best.

Our default recommendations: TCL for rentals, kids’ rooms and value-focused installs; Mitsubishi Electric MSZ for standard living areas and long-hold family homes; Daikin Cora or higher for master bedrooms and premium living areas. If you have a preference, we’ll match it.

Running-cost basics for a Perth summer

Rough guide for a modern inverter split system running an average 6 hours a day through peak summer at Perth’s current WA tariff:

  • 2.5kW bedroom unit — $25-45 per month peak summer
  • 3.5kW small living — $40-70 per month peak summer
  • 5.0kW medium living — $60-100 per month peak summer
  • 7.0kW large living — $80-140 per month peak summer

The single biggest running-cost lever isn’t brand or model — it’s inverter vs fixed-speed technology. All the modern TCL, Mitsubishi Electric and Daikin units we install are inverter-driven. If you’re replacing a 12+ year old fixed-speed unit, expect 40-60% lower running costs on the same cooling output. That’s often enough that replacement pays for itself in running-cost savings within 3-4 summers.

Tariff timing

WA has moved to time-of-use pricing for many households since 2024. Running the aircon during off-peak periods (typically overnight through mid-morning) costs meaningfully less than peak evening hours. Modern inverter units can be scheduled to pre-cool the house before peak — ask us about setup.

Common bundling: what else people upgrade at the same time

The switchboard is opened during a new aircon install to add the dedicated circuit — which makes it a natural moment to catch other things. Common bundles:

  • Switchboard capacity check. If your board is close to full, a new aircon circuit might tip it over. Worth checking capacity before install day. See our switchboard upgrade guide.
  • Whole-house surge protection. The aircon control board is one of the most expensive items in the house for a lightning-induced surge to fry. A whole-house SPD at the switchboard is a cheap add-on if we’re there. See our surge protection guide.
  • RCD upgrades. If some of your existing circuits aren’t RCD-protected, adding them while the board is open is efficient. See our 30-second safety switch test.
  • Second unit for a bedroom. Adding a second small unit in the same visit is often significantly cheaper than a separate install — the electrician and mechanic are already there.

Common questions about Perth aircon installs

Why should I book my aircon install in August rather than later?+

Perth aircon install demand more than doubles between August and November. August customers get pick of install dates. November callers wait weeks. Booking now means your system is installed, tested and ready before the first heatwave — not after it.

How big a split system do I need for my room?+

As a rough Perth rule of thumb: bedroom (10-15m²) needs 2.5kW, small living or kids room (15-25m²) needs 3.5kW, medium living (25-40m²) needs 5kW, large open-plan (40-60m²) needs 7-8kW. But room shape, ceiling height, insulation quality, orientation and glazing all shift the number. We size the right unit over the phone once you tell us the room dimensions and layout — oversizing costs more to run, undersizing means it never quite cools.

What does "electrician-led install" actually mean?+

Two things. First: the electrical side of an aircon install is done by a licensed electrician, not a refrigeration mechanic operating on a limited electrical ticket. Your unit gets a dedicated circuit, a Clipsal double-pole isolator, RCD protection where required, and full electrical certification — issued directly. Second: our own licensed team does the whole job, not a subcontractor. You call one number, one team turns up, one company warrants the whole install for life.

TCL, Mitsubishi Electric or Daikin — which one?+

We install all three as a good-better-best line-up. TCL is our value tier — solid inverter split systems at the best price, ideal for rentals, secondary bedrooms and budget-conscious upgrades. Mitsubishi Electric (MSZ) is our workhorse mid-tier — strong reliability, excellent parts availability in WA, simple controls. Daikin (Cora, US7) is our premium tier — quietest at the top end, best dehumidification. We'll recommend based on room, budget and features you actually want.

How much does it cost to run a split system through a Perth summer?+

Highly variable, but a rough guide: a 3.5kW inverter split system running for 6 hours a day at Perth's WA tariff typically costs $40-70 per month during peak summer. A 7kW unit for a larger living area, $80-140. Inverter systems are dramatically more efficient than fixed-speed — the running-cost gap between a modern Daikin/Mitsubishi and an old non-inverter unit is often 40-60%. If your existing unit is 12+ years old, replacement often pays for itself in running-cost savings within 3-4 years.

How does the fixed-price-over-the-phone process work?+

Ring us with the room dimensions, layout, where you'd like the indoor head mounted (usually high on a wall facing the seating area), and roughly where the outdoor compressor should sit. We give you a fixed price on the spot including the unit, install, dedicated circuit, isolator and certification. The walkthrough confirms it on site — if we spot something that changes the price (asbestos, structural obstruction, an existing outdoor spot that won't work), we tell you before any work begins. No surprise variations on install day.

Do I need to buy the unit separately or does that come with the install?+

We supply and install as one job. Buying the unit separately from a retailer (JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, etc.) then finding an installer usually costs the same or more once install is done, and it splits the warranty — the retailer warrants the unit, the installer warrants the install. When we supply and install, one company warrants everything: the unit under manufacturer warranty, the install for life on workmanship.
