Security Lighting Services

Every Security Light, Positioned and Installed Right

From single motion sensors to complete perimeter lighting with smart controls and battery backup.

Motion-Sensor Flood Lights

Halogen or LED flood lights with passive infrared sensors. Lights trigger when movement is detected within 10–15 meters. Perfect for driveways and entry doors.

Perimeter Wall Lighting

LED security lights around your property perimeter - corners, side gates, and garden beds. IP66/IP67 weatherproof housings rated for Perth's UV and heat.

Pathway & Deck Lighting

Low-level pathway and step lighting guides visitors safely and deters intruders from sneaking around gardens. LED-only for energy efficiency and cool operation.

Smart Switching & Timers

Motion sensors can be combined with photocells (dusk-to-dawn) and timers (lights on at specific times regardless of motion). Program your own schedule.

Switchboard Integration

All outdoor lights wired to your main switchboard with dedicated circuits, safety switches, and surge protection. Professional installation means no DIY failures.

Solar & Battery Backup

Solar-assist or standalone solar lights for areas where digging cable is impractical. Battery backup ensures lights work even during power cuts.

Perth's crime hot spots are unlit driveways and dark gardens. Proper security lighting costs less than one break-in claim.
