What We Do

Kitchen Electrical Services

Complete kitchen electrical work, from new appliance wiring to full kitchen rewires. Compliant. Professional. Done the right way.

Oven & Cooktop Hardwiring

Dedicated circuits, correct cable sizing, safety switches, and proper connections. Your oven and cooktop get their own power supply, isolated from other circuits.

Rangehood Installation

Sizing to meet NCC Part 10.6 minimum 50 L/s extraction. Ducting, damper, power connection, and testing all included. Your kitchen stays fresh and compliant.

Under-Cabinet LED Lighting

Low-voltage LED strips, hidden wiring, junction boxes. Brightens your bench work. Clean, professional installation with proper terminations.

Pendant & Decorative Lighting

Suspended lights over your island or bench. Proper ceiling routing, junction boxes, correct height (typically 800mm above bench). Looks professional and works reliably.

Dedicated Appliance Circuits

Individual 10-20A circuits for dishwashers, fridges, microwave, coffee machines. No sharing. No overloads. Complies with electrical standards.

Power Point Upgrades & Rewires

New power points, repositioning outlets, full kitchen rewiring. We handle old wiring removal, new cable runs, and modern socket placements to suit your layout.
