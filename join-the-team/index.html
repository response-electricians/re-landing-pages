Common Questions

Questions Before You Apply

What's the pay like?
We pay above-market rates because we charge premium prices for premium work. Exact figures depend on your experience and capability, but you'll discuss this directly in the phone conversation. No bait-and-switch.
Do I need my own tools?
You'll need your own basic hand tools (as any qualified sparky would). We supply all power tools, test equipment (Fluke gear), PPE, and specialist equipment. Your van will be fully stocked and ready to go each morning.
What hours do you work?
Standard hours are Monday to Friday. We roster fairly and respect your time outside of work. Overtime is available but never forced. We believe burnt-out electricians do poor work — so we don't burn people out.
I'm not in Perth yet. Can I still apply?
Yes. If you're a qualified electrician relocating to Perth and have the right to work in Australia, we're happy to chat. Select "Relocating to Perth" in the application form.
What if I'm currently self-employed or subcontracting?
Many of our best team members made the switch from contracting to permanent. If you're tired of chasing your own work, quoting jobs, and doing your own admin — we handle all of that. You just do the electrical work.
What makes Response different from other electrical companies?
Systems. We've built a business where electricians are supported, not just deployed. Job management, stock control, structured training, career pathways, and a leadership team that came from the tools. We've won Australia's Electrician of the Year twice and WA Service Business of the Year four years running — those results come from the team, not one person.
